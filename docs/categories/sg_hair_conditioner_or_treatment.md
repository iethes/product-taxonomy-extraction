## Scope — What's In vs Out

**In scope:**
- Hair conditioner (ครีมนวมเส้นผม, conditioner spray, leave-in conditioner, rinse-out conditioner)
- Hair treatment/mask (ทรีตเมนต์ผม, hair repair mask, hot oil treatment, protein treatment)
- Hair essence/serum (เซรั่มผม, hair oil, hair essence — when clearly marketed as conditioner line, not as standalone styling product)

**Out of scope (leave NULL):**
- Shampoo (แชมพู) — separate category, even if same brand
- Hair styling products (gel, wax, spray — ยาจัดแต่งผม)
- Hair growth / anti-loss products (โปรแกรมบำรุงผม, ยาปลูกผม) when marketed as functional/pharmaceutical
- Hair color/dye products (ยาย้อมผม) — separate category
- Hair washing/cleansing tools (แปรงสระผม, shampoo brush) — product category mismatch
- Multi-category bundles where conditioner is NOT the primary product

**Edge cases:**
- **"Hair+ 2 in 1 Shampoo + Conditioner"** — if marked as dual-purpose and clear from image that both functions are equally featured, include as conditioner-treatment line. If shampoo dominates and conditioner is secondary, route to shampoo category instead, or leave NULL if both are unclear.
- **Kérastase / Milbon / professional salon lines** — include all regardless of sub-line; these are clearly conditioner-treatment focused.
- **Oil-based treatments marketed as "conditioning oils"** — include if packaging/label clearly says conditioner/treatment. Exclude if purely styling/finishing oil.
- **Leave-in sprays** — include if labeled as conditioner spray; exclude if labeled as styling spray.

## Taxonomy Design Notes

**Product line extraction approach:**
- Most brands have clear sub-line names on packaging (e.g. Kérastase: Chronologiste, Elixir Ultime, etc.)
- Extract product_line directly from packaging text visible in images
- For multi-line brands (Shiseido, Pantene), differentiate by sub-line name in packaging
- For brands with unclear product line, write `"{Brand} (unresolved)"` and flag for review

**Brand disambiguation:**
- Shiseido Official Store and Shiseido Professional Official Store are same company but track different professional/consumer lines
- Some brands use parent company store names (e.g. Shiseido products via "BB Beauty Global Official Store")
- Use brand_from_image to confirm brand identity when multi-brand reseller merchants appear

**Size extraction notes:**
- Primary units: ml, g, L, oz
- Common sizes: 150ml, 200ml, 250ml, 300ml, 400ml, 500ml, 750ml, 1000ml
- Some premium brands (Kérastase, Olaplex) sell larger 300–500ml bottles
- Pack-count patterns: 
  - Single units (most common)
  - `"x2"`, `"แพ็คคู่"` for pairs
  - Resellers sometimes bundle multiple units (rare for conditioner vs body care)

**Known difficult products:**
- BRD-UNDEFINED products (67.86% of GMV) — most are reseller listings with no brand field; brand extraction from sku_name required
- Multi-size seller listings (seller offers choice of 150ml/250ml/500ml in one SKU) — use image to determine actual size, or mark `is_multi_size=TRUE` if ambiguous
- Some "hair essence" products marketed as both styling and conditioning — use image to confirm primary function
